Portfolio Manager 0.4.0: see how your stocks are connected.

Connections, on every stock
Open any stock (in Positions, Markets or a watchlist) and you'll find a new Connections section: a map with the stock in the middle and everything it's linked to around it.
- The indexes it's in, such as the ASX 50 or the S&P 500, with its rank and weight, and which of your other holdings are in the same index.
- Its competitors: the largest companies in the same industry, sized by how big they are. Start with its own market, or switch to All to include the other one (CBA's list then starts with JPMorgan and Bank of America).
- The people who run it, and its board of directors.
Anything linked to a stock you own is drawn in amber, including a director who also sits on the board of one of your holdings. Hover over anything for the details, click a stock to open it, and scroll to zoom.

The full map
Click "Open full map" for a bigger version on its own page.
- Click anything to see it in the panel on the right. For a person, that's their role, when they joined the board and the other company boards they sit on. Double click to open it.
- Depth 2 adds the other boards each director sits on.
- Find in map jumps to a name or ticker, and the list underneath shows every connection, with the ones that touch your holdings first.
- People open their Wikipedia page for now, when one is known. Proper profiles come in a later version.

Where the board information comes from
Boards are read by Claude from each company's own documents, and every name is checked against the document it came from.
- US companies: their yearly proxy statement filed with the SEC. The boards of the S&P 500, Nasdaq 100 and Dow companies come with the app, so they appear straight away.
- Australian companies: their annual report on the ASX. The ASX only allows its announcements for personal use, so the app reads an Australian board in your own copy, when you ask, after you accept that once. Reading a board costs about half a US cent on your Anthropic API key.

Also in this version

- Executives for every stock: names, roles, and pay and age where they're published.
